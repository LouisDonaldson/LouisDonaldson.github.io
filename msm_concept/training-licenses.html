Choose your licence

Our comprehensive training programs are designed to ensure you gain the necessary skills and confidence to ride safely and legally on the road. For anything other than CBT's, please get in touch for a tailored quote based on the licence you're working towards and the bike you'd like to train on.

A1

A1 License

For riders aged 17 and above, allowing you to ride light motorcycles up to 125cc.

A2

A2 License

For riders aged 19 and above, enabling you to ride motorcycles with a power output up to 35kW.

A

A License

The unrestricted license for riders aged 24 and above (or 21 and above with progressive access), allowing you to ride any motorcycle.

ERS

Enhanced Rider Scheme

Aimed at improving your riding skills and safety, suitable for riders of all levels.

DAS

Direct Access Scheme

For those aged 24 and above, providing direct access to larger motorcycles without the need for progressive licensing.
