Becoming a qualified instructor

Our team of professional grade A instructors are dedicated to providing personalised training tailored to meet your individual needs. With their extensive experience and expertise, they will guide you through every step of the process, ensuring you gain the skills and confidence required to succeed.

1

Theory

We provide a comprehensive learning app that includes all the latest DVSA questions and answers, as well as hazard perception videos required for the ADI theory test. This app allows you to learn all the necessary topics, practice sections you are unsure of, and take mock theory tests to prepare you thoroughly. You can study independently at no cost, but we also offer training support if needed to ensure you are fully prepared.

2

Driving

Our in-car training is conducted in our instructor vehicles, helping you achieve the driving proficiency required to pass your Part 2 test. You can also practice these skills in your own time, maintaining a high standard of driving essential for your new career. Once you have passed Part 1, you can apply for Part 2, taking the test in our vehicle or your own, provided it meets the test requirements.

3

Instruction

To develop your instructional skills in a safe and supportive environment, we start your training with you teaching your trainer. Once you feel confident, you can begin instructing real students with genuine training needs. After passing Part 2, you can apply for your Part 3 qualifying exam as soon as you are ready, using either our vehicle or your own for this test.
