About P&A Cleaning Services

Local, family-run, and dedicated to doing the job right.

Founders Photo Placeholder

Built on Trust, One Clean at a Time

P&A Cleaning Services started over a decade ago with two people, a van, and a commitment to doing right by every client. Today, our trained team serves homes and businesses across the region, but the promise hasn't changed: show up, do the work carefully, and treat every space with respect.

We're fully licensed and insured, and every team member goes through background checks and hands-on training before stepping into a client's home or office.
